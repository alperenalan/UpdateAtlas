Every app on your Mac. Every update, checked.

Update Atlas finds your apps, games, and command-line tools, then shows which updates are real and where to install them.

Download for MacView on GitHub

Free and open source. Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon.

Everything in one clear list.

See at a glance what is installed on your Mac and who is responsible for updating each item.

  • All your software

    Apps, games from Steam, GOG and Epic, Homebrew, npm and Python packages, and command-line tools, side by side.

  • Evidence, not guesses

    An app is marked up to date only after a reliable version check. Anything unverified stays clearly labeled.

  • Update the right way

    See who owns each update: the App Store, Sparkle, Homebrew, or the developer. Supported updates start only after you confirm.

  • Right in your menu bar

    A quiet update count in the menu bar, and notifications when new updates are confirmed.

  • Apps and what is inside them

    Extensions, plug-ins, and helper tools stay grouped under the app that manages them.

  • Private by design

    No account, no analytics, no ads. Update Atlas never uploads your software list.

Close at hand, even with the window closed.

Update Atlas can keep running in the menu bar, check on a schedule you choose, and open any update with one click.

At home in Terminal, too.

The bundled command-line tool returns the same inventory as JSON, ready for scripts and AI assistants.

$ update-atlas check --updates-only
$ update-atlas scan --offline | jq '.summary'
$ update-atlas skill > SKILL.md

Questions and answers

Is Update Atlas really free?

Yes. There are no ads, accounts, or paid tiers. Support is optional and never unlocks features.

Does it update my apps automatically?

No. Scans only read information. An update starts only when you choose it and confirm, and only when its owner can install it safely.

What leaves my Mac?

Your software list stays on your Mac. To check versions, Update Atlas contacts the official update sources of your apps and downloads its public software catalog from this site.

Does it run on Intel Macs?

Update Atlas itself needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later. It still lists the Intel apps installed on that Mac.

An app is missing or wrong. How can I help?

Open an issue on GitHub with the app’s name and where you downloaded it. The software catalog is updated independently of the app.
