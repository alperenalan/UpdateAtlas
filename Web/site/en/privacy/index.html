Privacy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Update Atlas is built to keep your information on your Mac. This page explains what the app reads, what it contacts, and what it never collects.

What stays on your Mac

To build its list, Update Atlas reads app information, package-manager records, and the folders you choose. The list, your settings, the update history, and the catalog cache are stored only on your Mac.

What the app contacts

To check versions, Update Atlas contacts the update sources of your software, such as developer update feeds, the App Store, GitHub, and package registries. Those services receive a normal web request, including your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

The catalog and app updates

Update Atlas downloads its public software catalog from this site at launch and every six hours, and checks this site for a newer Update Atlas when update checks are on or when you ask. These requests carry no information about your software.

What it never collects

There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking. Update Atlas never uploads your software list, and it never installs anything without your confirmation.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts. Your language and theme choices are saved only in your browser. Our host, Cloudflare, processes standard request logs. Buy Me a Coffee and GitHub Sponsors are separate services with their own terms.
